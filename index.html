Research Framework

Genesis Engineered Interpretation

Genesis Engineered Interpretation (GEI) is an independently developed alternative framework that examines Genesis 1–6 through language, literary structure, water systems, and engineering comparisons.

The Adam A-DAM hypothesis is one of the central proposals within this framework, exploring the possibility that familiar biblical descriptions may also be examined through a hydraulic and engineering lens.

GEI presents its connections as hypotheses offered for examination—not established conclusions of biblical scholarship, archaeology, or engineering. Readers are encouraged to compare the GEI interpretation with conventional scholarship and assess the evidence independently.

About the Book

Examine Before Explaining

Before You Go to Heaven concentrates on the opening sequence of Genesis—the first day, Genesis 1:1–5. The title is deliberately provocative: Genesis 1:8 introduces the naming of the firmament as Heaven on the second day. This volume asks what assumptions readers bring to the threshold before that naming occurs.

Readers will encounter conventional readings and GEI alternatives side by side. Sometimes the evidence supports a strong linguistic observation. Sometimes it permits more than one translation. Sometimes GEI offers a creative analogy that remains unproven. Those are different categories, and a serious investigation keeps them separate.

The book prepares readers to approach Day Two and the appearance of Heaven in Genesis 1:8 with sharpened attention to language, structure, and interpretive choices.
