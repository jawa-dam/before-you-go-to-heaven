# Before You Go to Heaven

**A provocative examination of Genesis 1:1–5 through language, literary structure, translation, and alternative interpretive possibilities.**

By **Wilbert Bouie Jr.**

## About the Book

*Before You Go to Heaven* concentrates on the opening sequence of Genesis — the first day, Genesis 1:1–5. The title is deliberately provocative: Genesis 1:8 introduces the naming of the firmament as Heaven on the second day. This volume asks what assumptions readers bring to the threshold before that naming occurs.

Readers encounter conventional readings and GEI alternatives side by side, examining homophones, homonyms, figurative language, parallelism, chiasmus, punctuation, translation choices, and more.

## Genesis Engineered Interpretation (GEI)

Genesis Engineered Interpretation is an independently developed alternative research framework examining Genesis 1–6 through language, literary structure, water systems, and engineering comparisons. Its hydraulic and engineering connections are hypotheses offered for examination, not established conclusions of biblical scholarship, archaeology, or engineering.
