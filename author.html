Wilbert Bouie Jr.

Independent Researcher & Founder of GEI

Wilbert Bouie Jr. is an independent researcher and the founder of Genesis Engineered Interpretation (GEI), an alternative research framework examining Genesis 1–6 through language, literary structure, water systems, and engineering comparisons.

His work explores the possibility that familiar biblical descriptions may also be examined through a hydraulic and engineering lens. The Adam A-DAM hypothesis is one of the central proposals within this framework.

Rather than presenting GEI as established academic consensus, Bouie invites readers and researchers to examine its claims, compare interpretations, investigate the language, and challenge the framework where evidence warrants.

His work is intended to encourage open inquiry and constructive engagement across biblical studies, linguistics, hydrology, and engineering.

Research Approach

Open Inquiry & Constructive Engagement

The GEI framework is built on a commitment to transparency. Every hypothesis is presented alongside conventional scholarship so that readers can evaluate the evidence on its own merits.

Bouie does not claim that GEI replaces or overrides established academic work in biblical studies, archaeology, linguistics, or engineering. Instead, the framework is offered as a set of interpretive possibilities that invite further investigation and interdisciplinary dialogue.

Research Transparency

Genesis Engineered Interpretation is an independently developed alternative interpretive framework. Its hydraulic and engineering connections are hypotheses offered for examination, not established conclusions of biblical scholarship, archaeology, or engineering. Readers are encouraged to compare the GEI interpretation with conventional scholarship and assess the evidence independently.
